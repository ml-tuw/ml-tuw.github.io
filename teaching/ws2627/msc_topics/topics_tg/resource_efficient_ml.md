title: Resource-Efficient Machine Learning
---

**Motivation**: Our environment suffers from the enormous energy consumed in data centers around the world for training and applying machine learning algorithms. Approaches to more resource friendly machine learning include low-precision computations, low-rank approximations, sparsification, and mixture-of-experts.

**Papers and topics**:

Potential topics include guarantees for approximate representations (Johnson-Lindenstrauss or Nyström), Expectation-Complete Representations, low rank computations (post-processing or training), and quantisation.

- Saha, Sagan, Srivastava, Goldsmith, and Pilanci. [Compressing Large Language Models using Low Rank and Low Precision Decomposition](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/a20e8451ffb07ad25282c21945ad4f19-Paper-Conference.pdf). NeurIPS 2024
