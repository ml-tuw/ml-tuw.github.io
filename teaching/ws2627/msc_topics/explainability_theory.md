**Motivation**:
Explanations are often used to promote trust in machine learning models, but they can be misleading if they do not faithfully reflect the model’s behaviour.
Verifying whether an explanation accurately describes a model’s behaviour can itself be a challenging task.
A theoretical foundation for explainable AI is therefore essential to establish what explanations guarantee and when they can be computed and verified efficiently.

**Papers and Topics**:
- Robi Bhattacharjee, Ulrike von Luxburg. "Auditing Local Explanations is Hard." NeurIPS 2024.
